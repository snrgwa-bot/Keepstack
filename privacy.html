Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

The short version: Keepstack is built to be private. Everything you save stays on your device — or, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own private iCloud account. We do not require an account, we do not collect personal data, and we do not track you.

This Privacy Policy explains how Keepstack (“the app”, “we”, “us”), developed by Essential Dev Team, handles your information. By using Keepstack you agree to this policy.

Information we do not collect

Keepstack does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No name, email, or contact information is collected by the app.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking SDKs are used.
  • We operate no backend server that receives your data.

Your saves

The links, notes, photos, screenshots, videos, audio recordings, and PDFs you save are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us. If you delete the app, its locally stored data is removed by iOS.

iCloud Sync (optional)

Keepstack offers an optional iCloud Sync setting, which is off by default. When you switch it on, your saves are synchronised through your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other Apple devices. This data is stored in your personal iCloud container, governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy — we have no access to it, and it is never sent to any server of ours. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time in the app’s Settings, and you can delete the synced copy along with your local data using the app’s delete-all-data control.

There is no Keepstack account

Keepstack has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. Nothing needs to be deleted from our side because we hold nothing: deleting the app removes your local data, and the delete-all-data control in Settings additionally removes the private iCloud copy if you enabled sync.

On-device AI

Keepstack’s AI features — reading text inside images, summarizing pages, auto-tagging, categorizing, and semantic search — run entirely on your device using Apple’s built-in frameworks (Vision and Natural Language). Nothing you save is sent to any AI service or server.

Saved web links

When you save a link, the app fetches that web page directly from your device to capture its title, preview image, and text so your save stays readable and searchable. This is an ordinary web request from your device to the page’s website; no information is sent to us.

Photos, camera and microphone

Keepstack asks for access to your Photos library only when you choose to import a photo or screenshot, and for your camera or microphone only when you choose to capture a video or record an audio memo. This all happens on your device. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Notifications

If you set a reminder on a save, Keepstack schedules a local notification on your device. No push server is involved and no notification data leaves your iPhone.

Share extension

The Keepstack share extension lets you save content from other apps. Shared content is written directly into the app’s private storage on your device; it is never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases and subscriptions

Keepstack offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (“Keepstack Pro”). All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

Keepstack is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date above.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at essentialdevteam@gmail.com.
